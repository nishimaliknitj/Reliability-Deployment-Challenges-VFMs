# Reproducibility Statement

## Overview

This repository accompanies the survey paper:

**Reliability and Deployment Challenges of Visual Foundation Models in Visual Computing: A Task-Centric Survey**

The purpose of this repository is to improve transparency, reproducibility, and accessibility of the resources discussed in the paper.

## Repository Contents

The repository contains:

* Survey manuscript
* Supplementary material
* Taxonomy documentation
* Benchmark references
* Literature review resources
* Reproducibility notes

## Literature Collection Protocol

The reviewed literature was collected through a structured search process covering:

* IEEE Xplore
* ACM Digital Library
* SpringerLink
* Scopus
* arXiv
* Semantic Scholar

Additional papers were identified through backward and forward citation tracking.

## Inclusion Criteria

Studies were included if they:

1. Address visual foundation models or their application to visual computing.
2. Are written in English.
3. Present methodological, empirical, or analytical contributions.
4. Provide information relevant to reliability, robustness, deployment, or evaluation.

## Taxonomy Reproducibility

The proposed task-centric taxonomy is documented in:

`docs/taxonomy_decision_rules.md`

Classification decisions are based on explicit decision rules to ensure consistency and reproducibility.

## Benchmark Information

All benchmark values reported in the manuscript are reproduced from the corresponding original publications.

Performance numbers are presented for comparative analysis and should be interpreted according to the evaluation protocols defined by the respective authors.

## Limitations

Some referenced models and datasets are subject to licensing restrictions and therefore cannot be redistributed through this repository.

Users are encouraged to obtain datasets, model checkpoints, and software implementations directly from the original authors whenever required.

## Contact

For questions regarding the survey or repository resources, please contact the corresponding authors listed in the manuscript.
